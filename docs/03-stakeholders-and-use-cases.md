# 03 — Stakeholders and Use Cases

| Stakeholder | Needs from StartupIQ |
|---|---|
| Startup founders / entrepreneurs | Understand their startup's readiness, strengths, weaknesses, risks; actionable recommendations; matched investors/incubators |
| Investors / angels / VCs | Evidence-backed startup assessment; investment suitability; startups matching their preferences |
| Incubators / accelerators | Incubation suitability; startups matching program criteria and eligibility |

## Matching Criteria
**Investor matching** may consider: industry, startup stage, investment size, geography, preferences, previous investments, risk characteristics.

**Incubator matching** may consider: industry, startup stage, geography, funding/support, mentorship, program criteria, eligibility.

Matches must be explainable (see `06-explainability-and-decision-engine.md`).

## Note
Stakeholder-specific requirements may feed the decision engine. Detailed per-role features are not yet defined (see `09-open-decisions.md`).
