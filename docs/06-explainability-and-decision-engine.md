# 06 — Explainability and Decision Engine

Explainability is a **core product requirement**.

## Every important recommendation communicates
- What the system concluded
- Why it concluded it
- Positive factors
- Negative factors
- Supporting evidence
- The distinction between **facts**, **predictions**, **rules** and **external research**

## Decision Engine
Produces the final action outcome (INVEST / INCUBATE / REJECT) by combining:
- ML predictions
- Deterministic rules
- Startup profile
- Stakeholder requirements
- Evidence

The outcome is a decision layer over a multidimensional assessment, not a raw classifier output.

## Matching
Investor and incubator matches must also be explainable (which criteria matched, which did not, and why).

Concrete decision logic and methods are undecided (see `09-open-decisions.md`).
