# Expense categoriser: model comparison

Generated by `python -m categoriser.train` (in `ml/`). Do not edit by hand.

## Data

Real UK MP office expense claims published by IPSA, mapped onto 14 startup expense categories (`ml/categoriser/taxonomy.py`). Split by financial year: train 2010-11 to 2021-22, validation 2022-23 to 2023-24, test 2024-25 to 2026-27. Training rows used: 326,256 (capped at 25,000 per category, of 782,429). Validation 136,232 rows, test 121,032 rows; 41% of test rows have wording never seen in training.

**Auto-filed** is the share of transactions the model files without asking, at a confidence threshold chosen on validation so that precision stays at or above 95%. For keyword rules it is the share with any keyword match.
