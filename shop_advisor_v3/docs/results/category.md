## Data

SYNTHETIC: 4200 businesses, 6 categories, split by business (train 2520, validation 840, test 840). Each shop is seen the way it might be uploaded: 35% date and sales only, 15% without returns, 25% weekly totals. 23 features: log_avg_bill, items_per_bill, log_item_price, return_rate, weekend_share, sunday_share, month_start_share, diwali_lift, m01, m02, m03, m04, m05, m06, m07, m08, m09, m10, m11, m12, volatility, zero_share, trend.
