What is missing from your startup story?

Describe your startup the way you would to an investor or an incubator. StartupIQ reads it and lists what a reviewer would still ask you.

Try an example:

The check only sees what you wrote. A gap means "not stated in this text", not "your startup lacks it". The three model-based checks are estimates, and the three rule checks look for plain evidence in your words.

How reliable is this?

Trained on 400 public Y Combinator company descriptions that I labelled against a six-point rubric, then tested with cross-validation (each description scored by a model that never saw it). The labels are one person's judgement, so scores measure agreement with that rubric, not startup outcomes. Descriptions you write may be longer or shorter than YC blurbs, which can shift results.
